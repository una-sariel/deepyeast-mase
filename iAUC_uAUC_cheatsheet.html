uAUC (iAUC · Eq.6)

Li & Engelhard, arXiv:2602.04775 · abs · PDF

English

Meaning

The conditional probability of a correct ranking on positive–negative pairs whose prediction intervals do not overlap (decisive pairs). Overlapping pairs are abstained and excluded from the denominator.

Formulas

Interval comparison
\[ I_1 > I_0 \iff L_1 > U_0,\qquad I_1 < I_0 \iff U_1 < L_0 \] (otherwise: overlap)
uAUC · Eq.(6)
\[ uAUC := \frac{\mathbb{P}(I_1 > I_0)} {\mathbb{P}(I_1 > I_0)+\mathbb{P}(I_1 < I_0)} \]

How to compute

  1. Obtain an interval \(I(x)=(L,U)\) for each sample.
  2. Enumerate positive × negative pairs.
  3. Count:
    • n_correct: \(L_+ > U_-\)
    • n_incorrect: \(U_+ < L_-\)
    • otherwise: overlap (abstain)
  4. \[ \widehat{uAUC} = \frac{n_{\mathrm{correct}}} {n_{\mathrm{correct}}+n_{\mathrm{incorrect}}} \]

Example from the paper (§5, Experiment 3)

Dataset: Pima Indians Diabetes. Intervals: bootstrap-based prediction intervals. Point-score baseline AUC ≈ 0.8306.

  • At a 90% confidence level, the model abstains on 33.7% of pairs (\(\mathrm{AR}=\mathbb{P}(\mathrm{overlap})=0.337\)), but achieves uAUC = 0.916 on the remaining decisive pairs — higher than the point-AUC 0.8306.
  • As the confidence level rises from 50% to 99%, the abstention rate increases while uAUC stays in about 0.83–0.94 and increases monotonically (Figure 4 in the paper).

Interpretation: wider intervals mainly turn previously decisive comparisons into overlaps (abstention), rather than creating many high-confidence ranking errors, so the retained rankings become more reliable.

中文

含义

在预测区间不重叠、因而可以明确排序的正负样本对上， 排序正确的条件概率。重叠的对弃权，不进入分母。

公式

区间比较
\[ I_1 > I_0 \iff L_1 > U_0,\qquad I_1 < I_0 \iff U_1 < L_0 \] （否则为 overlap）
uAUC · Eq.(6)
\[ uAUC := \frac{\mathbb{P}(I_1 > I_0)} {\mathbb{P}(I_1 > I_0)+\mathbb{P}(I_1 < I_0)} \]

计算方法

  1. 每个样本得到区间 \(I(x)=(L,U)\)
  2. 枚举正类 × 负类样本对
  3. 计数：
    • n_correct：\(L_+ > U_-\)
    • n_incorrect：\(U_+ < L_-\)
    • 其余为 overlap（弃权）
  4. \[ \widehat{uAUC} = \frac{n_{\mathrm{correct}}} {n_{\mathrm{correct}}+n_{\mathrm{incorrect}}} \]

论文中的例子（§5，Experiment 3）

数据：Pima Indians Diabetes。 区间：bootstrap 预测区间。 点估计基线 AUC ≈ 0.8306。

  • 在 90% 置信水平下，约 33.7% 的样本对被弃权 （\(\mathrm{AR}=\mathbb{P}(\mathrm{overlap})=0.337\)）， 其余可决对上 uAUC = 0.916， 高于点估计 AUC 0.8306。
  • 置信水平从 50% 升到 99% 时，弃权率上升， 而 uAUC 约在 0.83–0.94， 且单调上升（原文 Figure 4）。

解释：加宽区间主要把原本可决的比较变成 overlap（弃权）， 而不是制造大量“自信排反”，因此留下的排序更可靠。
